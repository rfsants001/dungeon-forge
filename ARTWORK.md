# Ilustrações da projeção

As imagens foram geradas com o ImageGen integrado e estão em `public/assets/`. Os retratos do ogro e as imagens de marca e ambientação vieram do protótipo validado.

## armadura.webp

Use case: illustration-story. Original landscape 16:9 painted dark fantasy tabletop RPG bestiary illustration for Dungeon Forge. Subject: a human-sized empty medieval suit of dark engraved iron plate armor, visibly hollow and animated by a faint ember glow from inside the visor, approaching down the ruined forge hall. Isolate one subject only, with an immediately readable silhouette, center-weighted composition, near-black charcoal and warm brown shadows, forge ember light, hand-painted polished book-illustration textures. The image will be projected to players when they encounter the creature. No writing, border, logos, interface or gore.
